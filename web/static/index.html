Scope & Eligibility

Final race
Final GC/time standings require completing all 21 stages. Stage-based analyses retain completed results from exited riders.
Through stage S
Cumulative stages 1–S; riders must complete every stage up to that point.
Selected stages
Performance over your selected stages and stage types, rather than full-race GC.
Ranking cohort
Overall keeps the full eligible ranking; filtered reranks the selected riders.

Time & Rankings

Adjusted time
Finish time − time bonus + time penalty. Times are shown as hours:minutes:seconds.
Young Rider
Riders born on or after 1 January 2000.
Per-stage top 3
Sum each team's three fastest riders on every stage; contributors can change.
Cumulative top 3
Sum the three fastest riders who completed every stage in the selected scope.
Points Proxy / KOM Proxy
Stage-finish and stage-type-based scores, never official sprint or mountain classifications. Tied scores share a rank.

Participation & Samples

Country participation
Rider nationality and team registration are different roles. Country summaries cover the whole race; nationality counts exclude unknown nationality.
Stage finishers
All riders who finished each selected stage; rider and team filters do not narrow this count.
Percentile & sample size
Higher is better; compare the number of completed stages alongside each average.
Native Stage Type Pivot
Full-race view. Use the adjacent Cross-Tab or Explore table for filtered comparisons.
Retired riders
Keep their completed stages and team contributions; incomplete totals are not final GC.

OLAP Operations

Slice & dice
Combine stage types, stages, teams, riders and age eligibility in Filters.
Roll-up
Compare stage results at rider, team, stage-type or race-week level.
Drill-down
Follow a team to its riders and individual stage results, or open a stage or country's members.
Pivot
Compare riders across four stage types in the Metabase matrix.
